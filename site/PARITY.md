# adhx.com design parity

The site (`site/`) checked against the design boards `site-1-desktop` (1440 × 6500) and
`site-2-phone` (390 × 6064). Captured with headless Chromium at deviceScaleFactor 1, with reduced
motion, at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 390, 375 and 320. Each section was compared side by side with
the rendered boards and by measuring every text box (position, size, weight, colour) in both.

No width scrolls sideways (scroll width equals the viewport at all seven widths).

Key: **match** is as on the board. **fixed** was off and is now as on the board. **deviation** is
kept on purpose, for the reason given.
